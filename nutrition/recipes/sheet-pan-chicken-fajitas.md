# Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

**Prep time:** 15 min + 30 min marinade (optional)
**Cook time:** 20–25 min
**Servings:** 4
**Equipment:** Sheet pan, oven
**Make-ahead:** Can slice and marinate chicken + veggies ahead
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — great reheated or cold in a bowl the next day

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced into strips | 1.5 lbs |
| Bell peppers (mixed colors), sliced | 3 peppers |
| Yellow onion, sliced into half-moons | 1 large |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Lime juice | 2 tbsp (about 1 lime) |

### Fajita Seasoning (or use 1 packet store-bought)
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Chili powder | 1 tbsp |
| Cumin | 1 tsp |
| Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| Onion powder | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Cayenne (optional) | 1/4 tsp |

### Toppings
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Avocado | 1 medium | Slice or make quick guac |
| Flour tortillas (large) | 4 | Calvin |
| Cilantro lime rice | 1 cup cooked | Calvin |
| Shredded cheese | 1/4 cup | Calvin |
| Sour cream | 2 tbsp | Calvin |
| Lime wedges | 1 lime, quartered | For squeezing |
| Fresh cilantro | garnish | |

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment.
2. Mix fajita seasoning ingredients together (or open packet).
3. In a large bowl, toss sliced chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, half the lime juice, and 2/3 of the seasoning. Set aside.
4. In the same bowl, toss sliced peppers and onions with remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and remaining seasoning.
5. Spread chicken on one half of the sheet pan, veggies on the other half. Single layer — don't pile.
6. Roast 20–25 minutes, tossing veggies at the 12-minute mark. Chicken is done at 165°F internal.
7. Squeeze remaining lime juice over everything when it comes out.
8. Serve:
   - **Mom:** Fajita bowl — chicken and veggies over nothing, topped with avocado and lime.
   - **Connie:** Fajita bowl or one small tortilla.
   - **Calvin:** Load up tortillas with chicken, veggies, rice, cheese, sour cream.

## Notes
- One pan, minimal cleanup — great for a Saturday night.
- The key is HIGH heat and not overcrowding — you want charred edges, not steamed chicken.
- Use two sheet pans if your pan feels crowded.
- Leftovers make excellent lunch bowls the next day.
