# Honey Garlic Pork Tenderloin

**Prep time:** 10 min + 1 hr marinade
**Cook time:** 20–25 min (grill or oven sear + finish)
**Servings:** 4–5
**Equipment:** Grill (or oven-safe skillet + oven)
**Make-ahead:** Yes — grill Sunday, slice and store
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — great cold on Mediterranean plates or reheated

## Nutrition (per 4 oz cooked serving with glaze)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 200 | 30g | 4g | 10g |

## Ingredients

### Marinade / Glaze
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Pork tenderloin | 1.5 lbs |
| Honey | 3 tbsp |
| Soy sauce (low sodium) | 3 tbsp |
| Garlic, minced | 5 cloves |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tbsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1/2 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp |

## Instructions

### Option A: Grill (recommended for Sunday prep)
1. Whisk together honey, soy sauce, garlic, olive oil, Dijon, paprika, pepper, and salt.
2. Reserve 2 tbsp of glaze in a separate bowl for finishing.
3. Place pork tenderloin in a zip-lock bag, pour remaining marinade over, and coat evenly. Marinate 1 hour or overnight.
4. Preheat grill to medium-high (400°F).
5. Grill tenderloin, turning every 4–5 minutes, until internal temp reaches 145°F (about 15–20 min total).
6. Brush with reserved glaze during last 2 minutes.
7. Rest 5 minutes, then slice into medallions.

### Option B: Sear + Oven
1. Marinate as above.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F.
3. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Sear tenderloin 2 minutes per side.
4. Brush with reserved glaze. Transfer skillet to oven.
5. Roast 12–15 minutes until internal temp reaches 145°F.
6. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.

## Notes
- Pork tenderloin is one of the leanest cuts — only 4g fat per serving.
- The glaze caramelizes beautifully on the grill.
- Slice thin for lunch containers — pairs great with rice for the kids or a Mediterranean plate for Mom.
