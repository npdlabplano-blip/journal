# Air Fried Green Beans with Everything Bagel Seasoning

**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 8 min
**Servings:** 3
**Equipment:** Air fryer
**Make-ahead:** Trim beans ahead if desired
**Leftover-friendly:** Best fresh, OK reheated

## Nutrition (per 1 cup serving)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 55 | 2g | 2g | 8g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Fresh green beans, trimmed | 1 lb |
| Olive oil spray | light coat |
| Everything bagel seasoning | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | pinch (the seasoning has salt — go easy) |

## Instructions
1. Trim ends off green beans. Pat dry.
2. Toss with olive oil spray and everything bagel seasoning.
3. Arrange in air fryer basket in a single layer (may need 2 batches).
4. Air fry at 400°F for 7–8 minutes, shaking halfway through.
5. Beans should be blistered and slightly charred at the tips.

## Notes
- The everything seasoning gives these a surprisingly addictive flavor — like a savory snack.
- Great side for burgers, steak, or grilled chicken.
- If you don't have everything bagel seasoning, mix: 1 tsp sesame seeds, 1 tsp poppy seeds, 1 tsp dried garlic, 1 tsp dried onion, 1/2 tsp salt.
