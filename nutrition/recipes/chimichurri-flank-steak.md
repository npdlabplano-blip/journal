# Chimichurri Flank Steak

**Prep time:** 15 min + 2 hr marinade
**Cook time:** 10–12 min on grill
**Servings:** 4–5
**Equipment:** Grill
**Make-ahead:** Yes — grill Sunday, slice and store
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — great cold or reheated

## Nutrition (per 4 oz cooked serving, with 2 tbsp chimichurri)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 280 | 32g | 17g | 1g |

## Ingredients

### Steak
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Flank steak | 1.5 lbs |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 tsp |

### Chimichurri Sauce (makes ~3/4 cup)
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped | 1 cup packed |
| Fresh cilantro, finely chopped | 1/2 cup packed |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Red wine vinegar | 1/4 cup |
| Extra virgin olive oil | 1/2 cup |
| Red pepper flakes | 1/2 tsp |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/4 tsp |

## Instructions

### Chimichurri
1. Combine parsley, cilantro, garlic, red wine vinegar, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
2. Stir in olive oil until combined.
3. Let sit at least 30 minutes for flavors to meld. Store in a jar — keeps 5–7 days refrigerated.

### Steak
1. Season flank steak on both sides with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
2. Optionally spoon a few tablespoons of chimichurri over the steak and marinate 2 hours.
3. Preheat grill to high (450–500°F).
4. Grill 4–5 minutes per side for medium (135°F internal). Flank steak is best medium or medium-rare.
5. Rest 10 minutes — this is important for flank steak.
6. Slice AGAINST the grain into thin strips.
7. Serve topped with chimichurri sauce.

## Notes
- Slicing against the grain is critical — flank steak will be tough otherwise.
- The chimichurri doubles as both a marinade and a finishing sauce. Make the full batch.
- Great for steak bites in the kids' lunch boxes with rice.
