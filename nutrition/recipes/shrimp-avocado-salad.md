# Shrimp & Avocado Salad

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 5 min | **Servings:** 1 (Mom only)
**Per serving:** 412 cal | 40g P | 21g F | 13g C | 7g fiber

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Large shrimp, peeled/deveined | 6 oz (raw weight) |
| Olive oil | 2 tsp |
| Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| Lime juice | 1 lime |
| Avocado | 1/2 medium, diced |
| Cherry tomatoes, halved | 1/2 cup |
| Red onion, thinly sliced | 2 tbsp |
| Green leaf lettuce | 2 cups |
| Fresh cilantro | small handful |
| Salt and pepper | to taste |
| Red pepper flakes | pinch (optional) |

---

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high.
2. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds.
3. Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook 2 minutes per side until pink and curled. Squeeze half the lime over the pan.
4. Arrange green leaf lettuce on a plate. Top with avocado, cherry tomatoes, red onion.
5. Add warm shrimp on top.
6. Drizzle remaining lime juice. Garnish with cilantro and red pepper flakes.

---

## Notes
- **Solo night dinner** — quick, light, satisfying.
- Pairs well with a sparkling water and some quiet time.
- Use the rest of the shrimp bag for a different night if desired.
