# Thai Peanut Chicken Lettuce Wraps

**Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 0 min (uses pre-grilled chicken) | **Servings:** 4
**Per serving (Mom's portion):** 406 cal | 34g P | 22g F | 15g C | 4g fiber

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Grilled chicken thighs (from Sunday prep), shredded | 1 lb |
| Butter lettuce | 2 heads, separated into cups |
| Carrots, shredded or matchstick | 2 medium |
| Cucumber, matchstick | 1 medium |
| Crushed peanuts | 3 tbsp |
| Fresh cilantro | 1/4 cup leaves |
| Fresh mint | small handful |
| Lime wedges | for serving |

### Peanut Sauce
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Creamy peanut butter | 3 tbsp |
| Soy sauce (low sodium) | 2 tbsp |
| Lime juice | 1 tbsp |
| Honey | 1 tbsp |
| Sriracha | 1 tsp (adjust to taste) |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove |
| Warm water | 2 tbsp (to thin) |

---

## Instructions

1. **Peanut sauce:** Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, sriracha, and garlic. Add warm water to reach drizzle consistency. (Can be made Sunday and refrigerated.)
2. Shred grilled chicken thighs with two forks.
3. Arrange butter lettuce cups on a platter.
4. Set out bowls of: shredded chicken, carrots, cucumber, crushed peanuts, cilantro, mint, peanut sauce, lime wedges.
5. Everyone builds their own wraps — assembly style.
