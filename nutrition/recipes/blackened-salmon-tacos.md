# Blackened Salmon Tacos

**Prep:** 10 min | **Cook:** 8 min | **Servings:** 4
**Per serving (Mom's — lettuce wraps):** 371 cal | 37g P | 19g F | 10g C | 4g fiber
**Per serving (kids — crispy shells):** ~480 cal | 37g P | 24g F | 26g C

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Salmon fillets, skin-on | 1.5 lbs (4 fillets) |
| Blackening seasoning | 2 tbsp |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
| Green leaf lettuce leaves (Mom) | 6 large leaves |
| Crispy taco shells (kids) | 1 box |
| Cilantro-lime slaw (from Sunday prep) | 2 cups |
| Lime wedges | for serving |

### Avocado Crema (No Sour Cream)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Avocado | 1 ripe |
| Plain Greek yogurt | 1/4 cup |
| Lime juice | 1 tbsp |
| Garlic powder | 1/4 tsp |
| Salt | pinch |

### Blackening Seasoning (if making from scratch)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Smoked paprika | 1 tbsp |
| Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| Onion powder | 1 tsp |
| Cayenne pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Dried thyme | 1/2 tsp |
| Dried oregano | 1/2 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |

---

## Instructions

1. **Avocado crema:** Mash avocado with Greek yogurt, lime juice, garlic powder, and salt until smooth. Refrigerate.
2. Pat salmon fillets dry. Press blackening seasoning onto both sides.
3. Heat cast iron skillet over medium-high until smoking. Add olive oil.
4. Place salmon skin-side up. Cook 3–4 minutes until a dark crust forms.
5. Flip. Cook 3–4 minutes until salmon flakes easily. Internal temp 145°F.
6. Remove skin. Break salmon into large chunks.
7. Serve in lettuce cups (Mom) or crispy taco shells (kids) topped with slaw and crema.
