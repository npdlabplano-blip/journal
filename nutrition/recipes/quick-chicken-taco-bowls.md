# Quick Chicken Taco Bowls (No-Cook Assembly)

**Prep time:** 10 min (assembly only — chicken and pico already prepped)
**Cook time:** 0 min
**Servings:** 3
**Equipment:** None — just bowls and a cutting board
**Make-ahead:** All components prepped Sunday. This IS the make-ahead meal.
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — components keep separately all week

## Nutrition (Mom's portion — no rice, light cheese)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 358 | 32g | 20g | 11g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Grilled lemon herb chicken (from Sunday), sliced or diced | 12 oz total | Already cooked |
| Taco seasoning | 1 packet or 2 tbsp homemade | Sprinkle over chicken |
| Romaine lettuce, chopped | 1 head | Base of the bowl |
| Pico de gallo (from Sunday prep) | 1 cup total | Already made |
| Shredded Mexican cheese | 1/2 cup total | Light for Mom/Connie, more for Calvin |
| Sour cream | 3 tbsp total | 1 tbsp each |
| Avocado, diced | 1 medium | Divide among bowls |
| Cilantro lime rice | 1–1.5 cups cooked | Calvin + small scoop for Connie |
| Tortilla chips | handful | Calvin only |
| Hot sauce (optional) | to taste | |

## Instructions
1. Warm sliced chicken briefly in microwave (1 min) or eat cold.
2. Toss chicken with a light dusting of taco seasoning.
3. Build bowls: romaine base → chicken → pico → cheese → sour cream → avocado.
4. Calvin: add rice and tortilla chips.
5. Connie: add a small scoop of rice.
6. Mom: skip rice, go light on cheese.

## Portions
| Person | Chicken | Rice | Cheese | Sour Cream | Avocado |
|--------|---------|------|--------|------------|---------|
| Mom | 4 oz | skip | 1 tbsp | 1 tbsp | 1/4 avocado |
| Calvin | 5 oz | 1 cup | 3 tbsp | 1 tbsp | 1/4 avocado |
| Connie | 3 oz | 1/2 cup | 2 tbsp | 1 tbsp | 1/4 avocado |

## Notes
- This is the Wednesday church night meal — needs to be on the table by 6:00 for a 6:20 departure.
- Connie loves tacos — this is her night.
- Can also be served as actual tacos in tortillas if you have them on hand.
