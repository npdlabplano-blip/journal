# Cilantro-Lime Slaw

**Prep:** 10 min | **Servings:** ~4 cups
**Per 1/2 cup:** 30 cal | 1g P | 0g F | 6g C | 2g fiber

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Green cabbage, shredded | 3 cups (about 1/4 small head) |
| Carrots, shredded | 1 large |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 1/4 cup |
| Lime juice | 2 tbsp (1 lime) |
| Rice vinegar | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Pinch of sugar | 1/4 tsp |

---

## Instructions

1. Toss shredded cabbage, carrots, and cilantro in a bowl.
2. Drizzle with lime juice and rice vinegar. Add salt and sugar.
3. Toss to combine. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving.

---

## Notes
- **Make on Sunday** — keeps 4–5 days refrigerated. Gets better as it sits.
- Perfect on fish tacos, in lettuce wraps, or as a side salad.
- No oil = very low calorie. All the crunch and flavor.
