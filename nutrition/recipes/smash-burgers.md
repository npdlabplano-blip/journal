# Smash Burgers

**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 15–20 min
**Servings:** 4 patties
**Equipment:** Cast iron skillet or flat griddle (stovetop)
**Make-ahead:** No — best fresh
**Leftover-friendly:** OK reheated, but best same day

## Nutrition (per patty with cheese, lettuce wrap — no bun)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 340 | 31g | 23g | 1g |

## Nutrition (per patty with cheese + bun)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 480 | 34g | 25g | 28g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Ground beef (80/20) | 1.5 lbs |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 tsp |
| American cheese slices | 4 |
| Butter lettuce leaves (for wraps) | 4–6 leaves |
| Hamburger buns | 2 (for Calvin) |
| Pickles, sliced | as desired |
| Tomato, sliced thin | 1 small |
| Onion, sliced thin | 1/4 medium |
| Yellow mustard | to taste |
| Ketchup (optional) | to taste |

## Instructions
1. Divide ground beef into 4 equal balls (about 6 oz each).
2. Heat cast iron skillet or griddle over HIGH heat. It needs to be screaming hot.
3. Place a ball of beef on the skillet. Using a sturdy spatula (or a burger press), SMASH it flat — really press it down hard for 5–10 seconds. Season the top with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Cook 2–3 minutes until the bottom is deeply browned and crispy. You want a hard sear.
5. Flip. Immediately place a slice of American cheese on top.
6. Cook 1–2 more minutes. The cheese will melt from the residual heat.
7. Remove to a plate. Repeat with remaining patties.
8. Serve:
   - **Mom & Connie:** Lettuce wrap — wrap patty in 2 butter lettuce leaves, add pickles, tomato, mustard.
   - **Calvin:** On a bun (or double up with 2 patties on one bun).

## Notes
- The key to a smash burger is the CRUST. Don't move it once you smash it — let it sear.
- 80/20 beef is important — leaner beef won't get the same crust and will dry out.
- Calvin's favorite — make him a double if he's extra hungry.
- Total cook time is fast: ~25 minutes from start to table including sides.
