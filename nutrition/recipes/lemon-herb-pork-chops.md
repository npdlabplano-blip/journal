# Grilled Lemon-Herb Pork Chops

**Prep:** 10 min + 30 min marinade | **Cook:** 12 min | **Servings:** 4
**Per serving (5 oz meat):** 220 cal | 36g P | 8g F | 0g C

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Bone-in pork chops | 4 chops (~2 lbs) |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 2 tbsp (1 lemon) |
| Lemon zest | 1 lemon |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Fresh rosemary, chopped | 1 tbsp |
| Fresh thyme leaves | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |

---

## Instructions

1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, zest, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
2. Coat pork chops in marinade. Refrigerate at least 30 min (up to 4 hours).
3. Preheat grill to medium-high (~400°F).
4. Grill pork chops 5–6 minutes per side until internal temp hits 145°F.
5. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
