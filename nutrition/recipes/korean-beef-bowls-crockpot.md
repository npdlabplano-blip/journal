# Korean Beef Bowls (Crockpot)

**Prep:** 15 min (Sunday night) | **Cook:** 8 hours on low | **Servings:** 6
**Per serving (Mom's — light rice):** 410 cal | 39g P | 13g F | 33g C | 5g fiber
**Per serving (kids — full rice):** ~580 cal

---

## Ingredients

### Beef
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Beef chuck roast | 2 lbs |
| Low sodium soy sauce | 1/4 cup |
| Gochujang paste | 2 tbsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tbsp |
| Brown sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tbsp |
| Rice vinegar | 1 tbsp |

### Bowls
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Jasmine rice (cooked) | 2 cups |
| Quick-pickled cucumber | 1 cup |
| Steamed broccoli florets | 1 bag (bagged pre-cut) |
| Green onions, sliced | 3 stalks |
| Sesame seeds | 1 tbsp |

### Quick-Pickled Cucumbers (from Sunday prep)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| English cucumber, thinly sliced | 1 large |
| Rice vinegar | 1/3 cup |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Sesame seeds | 1 tsp |
| Red pepper flakes | pinch |

---

## Instructions

### Sunday Night
1. **Quick pickles:** Toss sliced cucumber with rice vinegar, sugar, salt, sesame seeds, and red pepper flakes. Refrigerate in a jar. Keeps all week.
2. **Marinade:** Whisk soy sauce, gochujang, sesame oil, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, and rice vinegar.
3. Place chuck roast in a container or zip-lock. Pour marinade over. Refrigerate.

### Wednesday Morning
4. Transfer marinated chuck roast + all marinade to crockpot.
5. Cook on **LOW for 8 hours** (or HIGH for 4–5 hours).
6. Shred beef with two forks in the pot. Stir to coat in sauce.

### Assembly (~5:30 PM)
7. Cook jasmine rice (rice cooker).
8. Microwave broccoli florets per bag instructions.
9. Build bowls: rice → shredded beef with sauce → pickled cucumber → broccoli → green onions → sesame seeds.
