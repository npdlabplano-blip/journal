# Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken Thighs

**Prep time:** 10 min + 2 hr marinade (or overnight)
**Cook time:** 12–15 min on grill
**Servings:** 6 (great for meal prep)
**Equipment:** Grill
**Make-ahead:** Yes — grill Sunday, use all week for lunches and dinners
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — stores 5 days refrigerated

## Nutrition (per 5 oz cooked serving)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 230 | 28g | 12g | 1g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Boneless skinless chicken thighs | 3 lbs |
| Olive oil | 3 tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 3 tbsp (about 1.5 lemons) |
| Lemon zest | 1 lemon |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried) | 1 tbsp chopped |
| Fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried) | 1 tbsp chopped |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1/2 tsp |

## Instructions
1. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, thyme, rosemary, oregano, salt, pepper, and paprika in a bowl.
2. Add chicken thighs to a zip-lock bag or container. Pour marinade over and coat evenly.
3. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight for best flavor.
4. Preheat grill to medium-high (400–425°F).
5. Grill chicken 5–7 minutes per side until internal temp reaches 165°F.
6. Let rest 5 minutes before slicing.
7. Store sliced in airtight containers for the week.

## Notes
- Great sliced over salads, in taco bowls, or with veggies for lunch.
- Can also be cooked on a grill pan indoors if weather is bad.
