# Cilantro Lime Rice

**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 20 min
**Servings:** 6 (about 1/2 cup each)
**Equipment:** Medium saucepan
**Make-ahead:** Yes — batch cook Sunday, reheat through the week
**Leftover-friendly:** Yes — stores 5 days, reheats in microwave

## Nutrition (per 1/2 cup cooked)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 130 | 2g | 1g | 28g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Jasmine rice | 2 cups dry |
| Water | 3 cups |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Lime juice | 2 tbsp (about 1 lime) |
| Lime zest | 1 lime |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 1/4 cup |
| Butter or olive oil (optional) | 1 tsp |

## Instructions
1. Rinse rice under cold water until water runs clear.
2. Combine rice, water, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil.
3. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer 18 minutes. Do not open the lid.
4. Remove from heat, keep covered, and let steam 5 minutes.
5. Fluff with a fork. Stir in lime juice, lime zest, cilantro, and butter/oil if using.
6. Store in airtight container.
