# Air Fried Zucchini with Garlic and Parmesan

**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 10 min
**Servings:** 3
**Equipment:** Air fryer
**Make-ahead:** Best fresh, but can prep (slice) ahead
**Leftover-friendly:** OK reheated, but best same day

## Nutrition (per 1 cup serving)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 67 | 4g | 3g | 4g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Zucchini, sliced into 1/4" rounds | 3 medium |
| Olive oil spray | light coat |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Grated parmesan | 2 tbsp |

## Instructions
1. Slice zucchini into 1/4-inch rounds.
2. Toss in a bowl with olive oil spray, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket (may need 2 batches).
4. Air fry at 400°F for 8–10 minutes, shaking halfway through.
5. Sprinkle parmesan over the top in the last 2 minutes.
6. Serve immediately.

## Notes
- Don't overcrowd the basket — single layer gives you the best crisp.
- Can sub Italian seasoning for garlic powder for variety.
