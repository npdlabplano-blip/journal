# Air Fried Broccoli with Lemon

**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 10 min
**Servings:** 3–4
**Equipment:** Air fryer
**Make-ahead:** Can prep (cut florets) ahead
**Leftover-friendly:** Reheats well in microwave — good for lunch boxes

## Nutrition (per 1.5 cup serving)
| Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|----------|---------|-----|-------|
| 65 | 4g | 2g | 8g |

## Ingredients
| Item | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Broccoli, cut into florets | 2 heads (about 6 cups) |
| Olive oil spray | light coat |
| Garlic powder | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1/4 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp (about 1/2 lemon) |
| Lemon zest (optional) | 1/2 lemon |

## Instructions
1. Cut broccoli into bite-sized florets. Pat dry if washed.
2. Toss with olive oil spray, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Air fry at 400°F for 8–10 minutes, shaking basket at the 5-minute mark.
4. Squeeze lemon juice over the top and toss. Add zest if desired.
5. Serve immediately or store for lunches.

## Notes
- The edges should get a little charred — that's the good stuff.
- Works great packed into lunch containers with grilled protein and rice.
