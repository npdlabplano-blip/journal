Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken Thighs

Prep: 10 min + 2 hr marinade  |  Cook: 12–15 min  |  Servings: 6  |  Per 5 oz: 230 cal / 28g P / 12g F / 1g C

IngredientQty
Boneless skinless chicken thighs3 lbs
Olive oil3 tbsp
Lemon juice3 tbsp (~1.5 lemons)
Lemon zest1 lemon
Garlic, minced4 cloves
Fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)1 tbsp chopped
Fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)1 tbsp chopped
Dried oregano / Salt / Pepper / Smoked paprika1 tsp / 1 tsp / 1/2 tsp / 1/2 tsp
  1. Whisk marinade. Coat chicken in zip-lock bag. Refrigerate 2+ hours.
  2. Grill medium-high (400–425°F), 5–7 min per side to 165°F internal.
  3. Rest 5 min, slice, store in airtight containers for the week.

Chimichurri Flank Steak

Prep: 15 min + 2 hr marinade  |  Cook: 10–12 min  |  Servings: 4–5  |  Per 4 oz + chimichurri: 280 cal / 32g P / 17g F / 1g C

Steak

IngredientQty
Flank steak1.5 lbs
Salt / Pepper / Garlic powder1 tsp / 1/2 tsp / 1/2 tsp

Chimichurri Sauce (~3/4 cup)

IngredientQty
Fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped1 cup packed
Fresh cilantro, chopped1/2 cup packed
Garlic, minced4 cloves
Red wine vinegar1/4 cup
Extra virgin olive oil1/2 cup
Red pepper flakes / Oregano / Salt / Pepper1/2 tsp / 1 tsp / 1/2 tsp / 1/4 tsp
  1. Chimichurri: Combine all, stir in olive oil. Let sit 30 min. Keeps 5–7 days.
  2. Steak: Season, optionally marinate with chimichurri 2 hrs. Grill HIGH (450–500°F), 4–5 min per side for medium.
  3. Rest 10 min. Slice AGAINST the grain. Top with chimichurri.

Smoked paprika / Pepper / Salt1/2 tsp / 1/2 tsp / 1/4 tsp

Cilantro Lime Rice

Cook: 20 min  |  Servings: 6 (1/2 cup each)  |  Per 1/2 cup: 130 cal / 2g P / 1g F / 28g C

IngredientQty
Jasmine rice2 cups dry
Water3 cups
Salt1 tsp
Lime juice + zest1 lime
Fresh cilantro, chopped1/4 cup
  1. Rinse rice. Boil with water + salt. Reduce to low, cover 18 min. Don't open lid.
  2. Remove from heat, keep covered 5 min. Fluff, stir in lime juice, zest, and cilantro.

Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas

Prep: 15 min  |  Cook: 20–25 min  |  Servings: 4  |  Mom's bowl: 410 cal / 45g P / 17g F / 17g C  |  Saturday

IngredientQty
Chicken breast, sliced into strips1.5 lbs
Bell peppers (mixed), sliced3 peppers
Yellow onion, sliced1 large
Olive oil2 tbsp
Lime juice2 tbsp (1 lime)
Fajita seasoning2 tbsp (or 1 packet)

Toppings: Avocado (all)  |  Tortillas, rice, cheese, sour cream (Calvin)  |  Lime wedges, cilantro

  1. Preheat oven 425°F. Line sheet pan with foil.
  2. Toss chicken with 1 tbsp oil, half the lime, 2/3 seasoning. Toss veggies with rest.
  3. Spread on pan — single layer, don't pile. Roast 20–25 min, toss veggies at 12 min.
  4. Squeeze remaining lime over everything. Mom: bowl. Calvin: load up tortillas.
