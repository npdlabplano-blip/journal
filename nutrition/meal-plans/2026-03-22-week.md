## Beverage Plan

### Weekday Rule
- **Morning:** Coffee (black or minimal creamer) + 1 glass of water before each cup
- **Afternoon:** Diet Coke is fine + 1 glass of water before each one
- **Evening:** Sparkling water (Topo Chico, LaCroix, or Waterloo) — NO IPAs Mon–Thu
- **Goal:** 4-6 glasses of water/day just from the "water first" rule

### Weekend Rule (Fri–Sun)
- Same water rule applies
- **1-2 IPAs allowed** — budget ~220-250 cal each
- Stick to 1 if it's been a higher-calorie food day

### No Sweets
- No chocolate, ice cream, or dessert after dinner
- If cravings hit, pick one:
  - 1/2 cup frozen grapes (~55 cal)
  - 1 medium banana or frozen banana slices (~105 cal)
  - Herbal tea (vanilla or cinnamon, 0 cal)
  - Sparkling water
- Frozen banana slices taste like ice cream — keep a bag in the freezer
- Connie likes bananas too — good shared healthy snack
- This will get easier after 1-2 weeks as sugar cravings decrease
