Grilled Lemon-Herb Pork Chops

Prep: 10 min + 30 min marinade  |  Cook: 12 min  |  Servings: 4  |  Per serving: 220 cal / 36g P / 8g F / 0g C

IngredientQty
Bone-in pork chops4 chops (~2 lbs)
Olive oil2 tbsp
Lemon juice + zest1 lemon
Garlic, minced4 cloves
Fresh rosemary, chopped1 tbsp
Fresh thyme leaves1 tbsp
Salt & pepper1 tsp / 1/2 tsp
  1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, zest, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper.
  2. Coat pork chops. Refrigerate 30 min–4 hours.
  3. Grill medium-high (~400°F), 5–6 min per side until 145°F internal.
  4. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Calvin: double quinoa/rice. Leftovers slice cold for next-day salads.

Roasted Asparagus

IngredientQty
Asparagus, trimmed1.5 lbs
Olive oil1 tbsp
Salt, pepper, garlic powderto taste

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss asparagus with oil and seasoning on a sheet pan.
  2. Roast 10–12 minutes until tender-crisp and lightly charred.
  3. Squeeze lemon over before serving.

Avocado Crema

IngredientQty
Avocado1 ripe
Plain Greek yogurt1/4 cup
Lime juice1 tbsp
Garlic powder + saltpinch each
  1. Mash avocado crema ingredients. Refrigerate.
  2. Pat salmon dry. Press blackening seasoning on both sides.
  3. Cast iron skillet, medium-high, smoking hot. Add oil.
  4. Salmon skin-side up, 3–4 min. Flip, 3–4 min more. 145°F.
  5. Remove skin. Break into chunks. Serve in wraps/shells with slaw + crema.

Mom: lettuce wraps (saves ~120 cal). Kids: crispy shells + Colby Jack. Calvin gets rice on the side.
