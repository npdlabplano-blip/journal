# Weight Log

Daily weigh-ins, first thing in the morning before eating or drinking.
Weekly averages smooth out water weight fluctuations — focus on the trend, not any single day.

## How to Log
Add a row each morning. At the end of each week (Saturday), calculate the weekly average.

## Trend Notes
- First 1–2 weeks may show a larger drop (water weight). Don't expect that pace to continue.
- Weight can fluctuate 2–4 lbs day to day from water, sodium, hormones — this is normal.
- If the weekly average stalls for 3+ weeks, it's time to reassess calories or activity.
- Menstrual cycle can cause 2–5 lb temporary increases — track and expect it.
