# Body Measurements

Taken every 2 weeks, same time of day (morning preferred). Use a flexible cloth tape measure (the kind from a sewing kit — NOT a stiff metal one).
Measurements tell the real story — the scale can't tell you if you're losing fat and gaining muscle.

## General Tips
- **Wear:** Minimal clothing or the same fitted outfit each time
- **When:** Morning, before eating — same time as progress photos
- **Posture:** Stand tall, feet together, arms relaxed at your sides
- **Breathing:** Breathe out normally. Do NOT suck in your stomach.
- **Tape tension:** Snug and flat against your skin — not loose, not digging in
- **Consistency is everything:** Measure the exact same spot every time. Being off by even a little throws off the numbers.

## How to Measure Each Area

### 1. Waist
- **Where:** Around the narrowest part of your torso — this is usually just above your belly button, at the smallest point when you look in the mirror from the side
- **How:** Wrap the tape all the way around, keeping it level with the floor. Breathe out normally and take the reading. Do NOT suck in.
- **Why it matters:** This is the single best indicator of fat loss. If this number is going down, you're losing fat — even if the scale isn't moving.

### 2. Hips
- **Where:** Around the widest part of your glutes/butt
- **How:** Stand with feet together. It helps to stand sideways in front of a mirror so you can see you're at the widest point. Keep the tape level all the way around.

### 3. Bust
- **Where:** Around the fullest part of your chest, at the nipple line
- **How:** Lift your arms slightly to position the tape around your back and across your chest, then lower your arms and take the reading. Keep the tape level and straight — snug but not squeezing.

### 4. Thigh (Right Leg)
- **Where:** Around the widest part of your upper thigh — roughly the midpoint between your hip and knee
- **How:** Stand with your weight evenly on both feet. Wrap the tape around the thickest part. Keep it level.
- **Tip:** You can put your pinky on your knee and stretch out your thumb to find roughly the center of your thigh.
