# Body Measurements

Taken every 2 weeks, same time of day (morning preferred). Use a flexible tape measure.
Measurements tell the real story — the scale can't tell you if you're losing fat and gaining muscle.

## How to Measure
- **Waist:** At the narrowest point, just above the belly button. Relax, don't suck in.
- **Hips:** At the widest point around the glutes.
- **Bust:** Around the fullest part, keeping the tape level.
- **Thigh:** At the widest part of the upper thigh (measure both, log the right).
- **Arm:** At the widest part of the upper arm, relaxed (measure both, log the right).

## Notes
- Take measurements on the same day you take progress photos (every 2 weeks).
- Even if the scale stalls, inches lost means progress.
- Waist measurement is the single best indicator of fat loss progress.
