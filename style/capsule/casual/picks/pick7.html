3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Sunday Coffee Run
Scuba Half-Zip Hoodie (Burgundy) + Medium wash wide-leg jeans (#101) + White leather Keds sneakers (#510). Burgundy and medium wash denim is a foolproof casual pairing. The wide-leg jeans balance the oversized hoodie proportions — both have volume so it looks intentional rather than sloppy. Sneakers keep it clean.
Look 2 — Athletic Warm Tones
Scuba Half-Zip Hoodie (Burgundy) + Softstreme Jogger in Willow Leaf (Pick 1) + Black Converse All Stars (#512). Burgundy and sage green is a sophisticated warm-cool color pairing. Two Lululemon pieces together look coordinated. This is the soccer sideline look — comfortable enough to stand for two hours but cute enough for the team photo.
Look 3 — Game Day Go-To
Scuba Half-Zip Hoodie (Burgundy) + Medium-dark wash straight-leg jeans (#109) + White eyelet slip-on sneakers (#508). The deep burgundy against dark denim is a classic fall/winter color combo. The fitted straight-leg jeans balance the oversized hoodie silhouette. White slip-ons brighten the whole look. Cheering from the sidelines, looking great doing it.
