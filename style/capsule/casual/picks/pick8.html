Dolce Vita

Shore Sandals

A strappy flat sandal with the perfect amount of detail — buckle closures, thin criss-cross straps, camel suede that gets better with wear. Dolce Vita hits the sweet spot between trendy and quality. These aren't drugstore flip-flops but they won't break the bank either. The camel suede is a warm neutral that goes with absolutely everything casual in your closet.

Why This Pick

You have zero casual sandals — your only warm-weather shoe options are sneakers. For DFW summers, that's a real gap. The Dolce Vita Shore is a strappy flat with just enough detail (those buckle straps) to feel like a real choice rather than an afterthought. Camel suede is a warm neutral that bridges every color in your casual wardrobe — from jeans to linen pants to the new yellow floral dress. This shoe earns its $130.

3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Warm Neutrals Brunch
Issa Henley Tee (Pick 3, Clean Ivory) + Medium wash wide-leg jeans (#101) + Shore Sandals (Camel Suede). The camel sandal connects beautifully to the warm ivory tee and classic blue denim — a three-tone neutral palette that feels polished and intentional. Wide-leg jeans are perfect for a strappy flat sandal because the hem shows off the shoe.
Look 2 — Linen and Leather
Zoe Linen Pant (Pick 2, Safari Khaki) + Tan/white striped tank (#50) + Shore Sandals (Camel Suede). Warm earth tones in a summer-perfect combination. The camel suede of the sandals picks up the warm khaki of the linen pants. Strappy sandals peek out beautifully from under the relaxed linen hem. This is the definitive hot-Texas-day outfit.
Look 3 — Summer Statement
Yellow Floral Shirtdress (Pick 5) + Shore Sandals (Camel Suede). The easiest, most summery look in the capsule. Yellow floral dress plus camel sandals is a classic combination — the warm camel grounds the bright yellow beautifully. No overthinking needed — just put it on and go. Farmers market, outdoor dining, kid pickup in style.
