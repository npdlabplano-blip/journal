3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — After-Dark Casual
Black lace-trim tank (#60) + Reese Barrel Leg Pants + Black Converse All Stars (#512). Black top with oat barrel pants is a sophisticated contrast. The lace trim on the tank elevates this from basic — it's a look that works for casual dinner out or a movie night. The Converse keep it playful.
Look 2 — Weekend Neutral
Tan/white striped tank (#50) + Reese Barrel Leg Pants + White eyelet slip-on sneakers (#508) + Denim trucker jacket (#211). Warm neutrals with the tank, oat barrel pants, and a denim jacket as an easy layer. The warm tan stripe in the tank echoes the oat of the pants. A put-together look for a relaxed brunch.
Look 3 — Graphic Contrast
Black/white block stripe sweater (#63) + Reese Barrel Leg Pants + Light gray canvas sneakers (#509). The bold graphic stripe against the warm oat barrel pants is a classic contrast that always works. The wide leg of the pants balances the volume of the sweater for a proportional, editorial silhouette.
