Why This Pick

Yellow is one of the biggest color gaps in your wardrobe, and this warm buttery shade is universally flattering without being loud. The smocked texture adds visual interest and a bit of structure at the waist, while the flutter sleeves are playful and office-appropriate. At $32 on one of Loft's perpetual sales, it's practically free — and it pairs with every single trouser in this capsule.

3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Sunshine & Sage
Dried Moss Jayne trouser (BC Pick #2) + Sage/teal blazer (#205) + Brown pebbled booties (#504). Yellow and olive green is a gorgeous earth-tone pairing — warm, natural, and effortlessly chic. The sage blazer bridges the colors while the brown booties keep it grounded.
Look 2 — Classic Navy Contrast
Dark navy straight-leg pants (#125) + Navy ponte blazer (#203) + Black gold-horsebit loafers (#520). The buttery yellow pops beautifully against a navy foundation — it's a classic color combination that reads polished and intentional. The gold hardware on the loafers echoes the warm yellow.
Look 3 — Casual Taupe Friday
Mushroom Taupe wide-leg pant (BC Pick #1) + Denim trucker jacket (#211) + White Keds (#510). The flutter top tucked into the wide-leg trouser with a denim jacket thrown over is the perfect casual Friday formula. Yellow and taupe together is warm, approachable, and completely unstudied.
