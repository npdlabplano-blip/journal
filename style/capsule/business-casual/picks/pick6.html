Why This Pick

This is the warm neutral workhorse your capsule needs. Cafe au lait is the color equivalent of a perfect latte — it flatters every skin tone and pairs back to every trouser in this capsule and your closet. The shawl collar wrap silhouette is one of the most figure-flattering designs out there: it creates a V at the neckline (no neck constriction), defines the waist, and drapes gracefully over 41" hips. At under $45 on sale, this is a steal for a piece that looks far more expensive than its price tag.

3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Olive & Latte Tonal
Olive Brown Daydrift trouser (BC Pick #3) + Dusty mauve blazer (#206) + Brown pebbled booties (#504). Cafe au lait + olive brown + dusty mauve is a rich earth-tone trio that looks effortlessly expensive. The wrap top's warm neutral bridges the olive trouser and mauve blazer perfectly. This is a "people will ask where you got that outfit" kind of look.
Look 2 — Ecru Sophisticate
WHBM Sequin Pinstripe pant (BC Pick #7) + Maroon blazer (#202) + Black gold-horsebit loafers (#520). The cafe au lait wrap top paired with the ecru sequin pinstripe creates a tonal warm palette, while the maroon blazer adds a rich jewel-tone contrast. The subtle shimmer of the pinstripe pant elevates the whole outfit for a client-facing day.
Look 3 — Casual Friday Ease
Dark navy straight-leg pants (#125) + Denim trucker jacket (#211) + White Keds (#510). The sophisticated wrap silhouette meets casual Friday energy. The denim jacket thrown over the shawl collar top is unexpected and cool — it says "I didn't try that hard" when you clearly did. Navy pants ground the warm neutral while white sneakers keep the whole thing relaxed.
