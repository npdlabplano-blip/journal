3 Ways to Wear It

Look 1 — Relaxed Friday Chic
Mushroom Taupe BR wide-leg (Pick #1) + Denim trucker jacket (#211) + White Keds (#510). The smooth taupe satin blouse elevates the khaki trousers while the denim jacket and white sneakers dial the energy back to Friday. Effortlessly chic without trying.
