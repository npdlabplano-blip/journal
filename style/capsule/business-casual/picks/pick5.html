3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Jewel-Toned Commanding
Black wide-leg trousers (#106) + Maroon 41 Hawthorn blazer (#202) + Black kitten heels (#517). Teal + maroon is an unexpectedly powerful jewel-tone combination. The black trousers ground the rich colors and the kitten heels keep it sophisticated.
Look 2 — Mushroom Taupe Contrast
Mushroom Taupe BR wide-leg (Pick #1) + Light tan/beige blazer (#208) + Brown pebbled booties (#504). The teal top pops against warm khaki — cool meets warm in the best possible way. The tan blazer and brown booties keep the palette grounded and cohesive.
Look 3 — Denim & Jewel Tones
Medium wash wide-leg jeans (#101) + Navy Skies Are Blue ponte blazer (#203) + Black Converse (#512). Business casual Friday meets cool-girl energy. The navy blazer ties back to the teal while denim and Converse keep the whole thing from feeling precious.
