Why This Pick

Your wardrobe is heavy on navy and black — this vivid green injects life into your work rotation without being loud. The split V-neck is open and flattering (no constriction around the neck), the 3/4 sleeves are office-appropriate for DFW spring and summer, and the relaxed body skims past the hip without clinging. At $59, it's a solid value for a piece that will pair with at least 6 different bottoms you already own. May want a nude or black cami underneath depending on how low the split sits.

3 Ways to Wear It

Styled with pieces already in your closet

Look 1 — Green on Green Power Move
Dried Moss Jayne trouser (BC Pick #2) + Sage/teal open-front blazer (#205) + Black gold-horsebit loafers (#520). Tonal green dressing that reads intentional and elevated. The vivid green blouse pops against the muted dried moss trouser while the sage blazer ties it all together. The gold hardware on the loafers adds a polished finishing touch.
Look 2 — Warm Neutral Ground
Mushroom Taupe wide-leg pant (BC Pick #1) + Light tan blazer (#208) + Taupe Rafe loafer mule (BC Pick #8). Green against warm taupe is an unexpectedly chic combination — the blouse becomes the focal point while the neutral base keeps everything grounded. The tan blazer and taupe mules create a cohesive warm palette that lets the green sing.
Look 3 — Boardroom Bold
Black wide-leg trousers (#106) + Black single-button blazer (#207) + Black kitten heels (#517). The all-black foundation makes the vivid green blouse the undeniable star. This is a power look for presentations, client meetings, or any day you want to command the room. Simple, sharp, unforgettable.
