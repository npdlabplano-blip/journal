# Outfit Log

Track what Nicole wears each day to avoid repeats and surface underused pieces.

## Rules
- **No repeat outfits within 3 weeks** — same top+bottom combo counts as a repeat
- **No repeat key piece within 1 week** — don't suggest the same statement piece (dress, blazer) two days in a row or even within the same week
- **Track wear counts** — helps identify overused favorites and forgotten pieces
- **Feedback column** — Nicole can rate outfits to inform future suggestions
