# Custody Schedule

Derived from the Agreed Final Decree of Divorce (filed April 2025, 303rd Judicial District, Dallas County).
Reference PDF: `reference/AGREED FINAL DECREE OF DIVORCE (4).pdf`

## Regular School Year — Parents Within 100 Miles

### Father's Possession (Robert)
- **Every Thursday overnight** — from school dismissal Thursday → school resumption Friday
- **1st, 3rd, and 5th weekends** — from school dismissal Friday → school resumption Monday
  - If not during school term: 6:00 PM Friday → 6:00 PM Sunday
- **Weekend extended by holiday** — if a student holiday/teacher in-service falls on the Friday before or Monday after a possession weekend, possession extends to include that day

### Mother's Possession (Nicole) — Default/Primary
- **All other times** not designated to Father
- Monday nights, Tuesday nights, Wednesday nights (every week)
- 2nd and 4th weekends (Friday through Sunday)
- Thursday daytime (kids return after school Thursday, go to Dad after school dismissal)

## Summer Schedule

- **Week-on / week-off** alternating, switching Fridays at 6:00 PM
- **Even-numbered years (2026, 2028...):** Dad starts with first week of summer
- **Odd-numbered years (2027, 2029...):** Mom starts with first week of summer
- Continues alternating until the Friday before school starts

### 2026 Summer (Even Year)
Dad gets the first week. Then alternating:
- Week 1: Dad
- Week 2: Mom
- Week 3: Dad
- Week 4: Mom
- *(continues alternating)*

**Meal planning impact:** Mom cooks for 1 (herself) on Dad's weeks, cooks for 3 on her weeks.

## Holiday Overrides

Holidays override the regular schedule. These take priority over weekends and Thursdays.

### Christmas
- **Even years (2026, 2028...):** Mom gets school dismissal → noon Dec 28; Dad gets noon Dec 28 → day before school resumes
- **Odd years (2027, 2029...):** Dad gets school dismissal → noon Dec 28; Mom gets noon Dec 28 → day before school resumes

### Thanksgiving
- **Odd years (2027, 2029...):** Mom gets school dismissal → 6 PM Sunday after Thanksgiving
- **Even years (2026, 2028...):** Dad gets school dismissal → 6 PM Sunday after Thanksgiving

### Spring Break
- **Even years (2026, 2028...):** Dad gets entire spring break
- **Odd years (2027, 2029...):** Mom gets entire spring break (Mother has superior right)

### Mother's Day
- Mom always gets Friday school dismissal → Monday school resumption

### Father's Day
- Dad always gets Friday 6 PM → Monday 8 AM

### Child's Birthday
- If the non-custodial parent doesn't already have possession, they get 6:00–8:00 PM on the birthday (picks up and returns to other parent's home)

## Notes for Agents
- **Nutrition Coach:** Use this schedule to determine headcount for meal plans. Default school-year pattern: 3 people Mon–Wed, 1 person (Mom only) Thursday, weekends vary by week of month.
- **Planner:** Factor custody transitions into daily routines (Thursday after-school pickup by Dad, Friday transitions on Dad's weekends).
- When in doubt, Mom has possession — she holds all undesignated time.
