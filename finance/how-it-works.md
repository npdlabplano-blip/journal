## The One Number That Matters

### $400/week = Fun Money

This covers ALL discretionary spending:
- Dining out, coffee, delivery
- Shopping (Amazon, clothing, Target, Home Depot)
- Personal care (hair, nails, Ulta, massage)
- Entertainment (events, movies, swing dance)
- Anything that isn't a bill or a grocery run

**Don't track categories. Track one number.**

If you're about to tap the card, ask: *"Am I still under $400 this week?"*

## Rules to Live By

1. **Autopilot everything possible.** If it's the same every month, it should auto-pay.
2. **One number.** $400/week. That's it.
3. **Pay Capital One twice a month.** 1st and 16th. Never carry a balance.
4. **Pay yourself first.** Savings transfers happen right after payday, not at the end.
5. **Travel is separate.** $300/mo set aside — don't count trips against weekly fun money.
6. **No shame.** Over budget one week? Tighten up the next. It's a rhythm, not a punishment.
7. **Monthly review.** 15 minutes with Finance Coach. Download CSVs, get the real numbers, adjust.
