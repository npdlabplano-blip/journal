## Scripture: Esther 1:1-4 (NIV)

> This is what happened during the time of Xerxes, the Xerxes who ruled over 127 provinces stretching from India to Cush: At that time King Xerxes reigned from his royal throne in the citadel of Susa, and in the third year of his reign he gave a banquet for all his nobles and officials. The military leaders of Persia and Media, the princes, and the nobles of the provinces were present. For a full 180 days he displayed the vast wealth of his kingdom and the splendor and glory of his majesty.

## A Kingdom Beyond Imagination

Can you picture this? King Xerxes throwing a party that lasted *six months*. Not a weekend celebration or even a week-long event, but 180 days of showing off his wealth and power. This wasn't just any king either—he ruled over 127 provinces spanning from India to Ethiopia. That's like ruling from New York to California, then adding most of the rest of the world on top of it.

As single moms, we often feel like we're barely holding our own little "kingdom" together—managing work deadlines, teenagers' schedules, household responsibilities, and maybe squeezing in five minutes for ourselves if we're lucky. Reading about Xerxes' massive empire and extravagant display might feel overwhelming or even discouraging.

But here's what's beautiful about starting the book of Esther this way: God is setting up a story where He's going to work behind the scenes in the most powerful kingdom on earth. The same God who had His hand on events in Xerxes' palace has His hand on the details of your life too—even when (especially when) things feel chaotic and out of your control.

## Reflection Questions

1. When you look at your daily responsibilities, what feels most "out of control" right now? How might God be working even in those overwhelming moments?

2. Xerxes was focused on displaying his wealth and power. What are you tempted to rely on or show off when you feel insecure about your circumstances as a single parent?

3. God was preparing to use an ordinary Jewish girl named Esther in this massive kingdom. What "ordinary" qualities or experiences in your life might God want to use for His purposes?

## Prayer Prompt

*"God, sometimes my world feels so small and chaotic compared to the big things happening around me. Help me remember that You see every detail of my life—from the teenage drama to the work stress to the bills on my counter. Just like You had a plan in Xerxes' kingdom, I trust that You have a plan for my little corner of the world too. Show me today how You're working, even when I can't see it."*

## This Week's Application

- **Trust in the unseen**: When your teenagers push boundaries or work gets stressful, remember that God is orchestrating details you can't see.
- **Embrace your "ordinary"**: Don't underestimate how God might want to use your everyday experiences of parenting, working, and navigating life as a single mom for His greater purposes.
