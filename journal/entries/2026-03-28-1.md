AI cannot replace you. 
Let me say that again.
AI cannot replace you.

Can it replace jobs? yes. But it cannot replace you.

Recently, I was in a meeting where we are working through a complicated design. Its new-frontier level stuff. And we are wrapping our heads around how we create an elegant design, which has all of necessary controls that are required.

Every environment is complex and involves a lot of different teams to get things done. Each team has requirements and controls. Each team has processes that work independently. Each team has a personality and is quite opinionated on how far they will bend their processes to support the work. All of this will have to be taken into account when considering the design. In the AI world, we call this "context".

Context is all of the components external to the ask that you know. Its the historical knowledge. Its the scars from failed projects. Its the personalities and the politics. You know how to work the system, the AI bot does not.
