# Prayer 03-29
This weekend has been tough. No doubt about it. In part, yesterday I got very little rest. But really the big issue was impact was the boundaries workshop. It got my head spinning and spinning and spinning about how I create boundaries. My mom, it’s pretty clear. Igor is so much harder. In many ways, he gives me things I have never had before. But in so many ways, he leaves me lacking. Worst yet, if I follow through with my feelings, those are labels I will never be able to undo. 

It’s hard to not feel so tied to him. Even this last week, he got crazy jealous about Nick. I reassured him, and he settled down. But after all of that, I was still left alone. I still had to spend the weekend by myself. No word from him. Somehow, he can be jealous when I even hint of another male friend, but he is allowed to spend the weekend with his wife. It really does not seem fair.

I in so many ways wish he prioritized me. Someways, I have convinced myself that he does. But the reality is, it isn’t true. I am of no value outside of mid-week entertainment. My heart cannot take more of this. This back and forth. The loneliness. All of it. It’s not even that I want another man in my life. I really don’t. But I really hate having my emotions fucked around with, and I feel like that is what he is doing.

How do I set boundaries?

God, what are the boundaries to set in place?

How do I be free from this pain that I feel? How do I be free so that I can love again? How do I find the right kind of love that you want me to have? Not this fake kind of love. Lord help me
