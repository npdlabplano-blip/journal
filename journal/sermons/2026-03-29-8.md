**God's incredible rescue: From spiritual death to abundant life through His grace alone**

## Historical Context

Paul wrote this letter while under house arrest in Rome (around AD 60-62), addressing both Jewish and Gentile believers in Ephesus. The Ephesian church was planted during Paul's third missionary journey and had grown into a strong, diverse community. In this passage, Paul is reminding them—and us—of the dramatic transformation God works in every believer's life.

## Supporting Points

### 1. **Our Desperate Condition (vv. 1-3): We Were Dead**
- **Spiritually dead** - not just sick or wounded, but completely dead in sin
- **Enslaved** - following the world's pattern and Satan's influence
- **Deserving judgment** - under God's righteous wrath

*Think about it: When you feel overwhelmed as a mom, remember this isn't about your performance—we all started in the same desperate place.*

### 2. **God's Incredible Intervention (vv. 4-7): He Made Us Alive**
- **"But God..."** - the most beautiful phrase reversal in Scripture
- **Motivated by love and mercy** - not our goodness, but His character
- **Raised us up with Christ** - we're already seated in heavenly places
- **Future display** - His grace will be showcased throughout eternity

### 3. **The Method: Grace Alone (vv. 8-10)**
- **By grace through faith** - completely God's work
- **Not by works** - takes the pressure off our performance
- **God's handiwork** - we are His masterpiece, His poem (Greek: *poiema*)
- **Created for good works** - not TO BE saved, but BECAUSE we're saved

### 4. **The Reminder (v. 11): Remember Where You Came From**
- **Don't forget** - remember your former condition
- **Former outsiders** - Gentiles were excluded from God's covenant people
- **Now included** - the stage is set for the unity message that follows
